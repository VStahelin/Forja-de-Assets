# Style: dark-fantasy

## Summary of the theme

- Palette: abyssal blue-black steel, tarnished gold fittings, blood-red gems, ember-orange runes, ink outlines.
- Strokes: `ribbon` family for heavy outlines (`blade` miter for sharp parts, `bold` round for soft parts), `clean` for engraved detail.
- Textures: hatch for steel shading, grit for battle wear, stipple for tarnish.

## Specifications the theme cannot express

- Resolution and export scale: vector, logical size per asset, exported at `scale: 2`.
- Edge treatment: soft vector edges (anti-aliased), heavy ink outline on every silhouette.
- Layering rules: blade under guard, guard under gems; glow (runes, gems) uses shadow only on the glowing part.
- Light: from the top-left. Left half of every metal part is lit, right half in shade.
- Density: high. Every large surface carries at least one of: bevel, hatching, engraving, wear.
- No baked text. Runes are abstract glyph shapes, never letters.
